Learn Blockchain Basics

This page explains blockchain in a simple way for students and beginners. It focuses on the main ideas without making the topic too complicated.

What does blockchain mean?

Blockchain is a digital record system. Information is stored in blocks, and each block is connected to the one before it. This makes the records harder to change without everyone noticing.

Instead of one company or person controlling the whole database, copies can be shared across many computers. This is why blockchain is often described as decentralised.

Main blockchain features

  • Decentralisation
    • No single point controls all records.
  • Transparency
    • Transactions can be checked by users on the network.
  • Security
    • Cryptography helps protect the data.

Why it matters

Blockchain matters because it can help people trust digital information, especially when different organisations need to share records safely.
