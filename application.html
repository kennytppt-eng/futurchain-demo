Blockchain Applications

Blockchain is not only used for cryptocurrency. It can support different industries by improving trust, transparency and record keeping.

Application comparison table

Industry Example Use Main Benefit Challenge

Finance

Blockchain can support cryptocurrency payments and decentralised finance services without relying fully on banks.

Supply Chains

Products can be tracked from origin to delivery, making it easier to spot fraud or missing information.

Healthcare

Medical records can be stored and shared more safely between trusted organisations.
