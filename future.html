The Future of Blockchain

The future of blockchain could include Web3, smart cities, digital currencies and better data ownership for users.

Future possibilities

  • Web3
    • Users could have more control over their online data.
  • Smart cities
    • Connected services could share trusted information.
  • AI and IoT
    • Blockchain could help secure automated devices and decisions.

My view

I think blockchain will become more useful when people understand it better and when it is used to solve real problems instead of just being treated as a trend.

For this website, I wanted the future page to feel simple and positive, while still showing that there are challenges before the technology becomes common everywhere.
